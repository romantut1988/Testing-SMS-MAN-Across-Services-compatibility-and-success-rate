### Do free SMS verification numbers still work?

Sometimes. Free numbers can work for smaller services and basic testing, but they are frequently reused and may already be blocked by major platforms. Public inboxes also mean that incoming verification messages are not private.

### Are paid SMS verification numbers guaranteed to work?

No. An **sms-activate review** should not treat any provider as having a guaranteed success rate. Platforms can reject numbers based on country, carrier, previous usage, number type, or their own verification rules.

### What is the difference between a one-time number and a rental number?

A one-time number is intended for a single verification. A rental number remains available for a defined period and can be more suitable for accounts that may need future SMS codes.

### Are non-VoIP numbers better for verification?

They can have broader compatibility with services that reject VoIP ranges. However, non-VoIP status does not guarantee acceptance. Platform-specific restrictions can still apply.

Account-based providers use accounts to manage balances, purchases, verification history, refunds, and rentals. SMSPool, SMS-Man, and TextVerified follow this general model.

### Can a verification number be used for account recovery?

Only while you retain access to the number. This is one reason rental numbers can be more appropriate for accounts that require ongoing SMS access. Losing a one-time number can also mean losing the ability to receive future codes.

### Which factors should be compared before buying a number?

In this **sms-activate review**, the main factors are platform compatibility, country, number type, price, privacy, rental duration, payment method, and the provider's policy when verification fails.

### Should I choose a free or paid number?

For basic testing, a free number may be sufficient. For private verification, repeated access, or an account you intend to keep, a paid number provides more control. The appropriate choice depends on the platform and how long you need access to the number.
